# ISO/IEC 27001 Mapping (reference)

ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A groups information-security controls into four themes: Organizational (A.5), People (A.6), Physical (A.7), and Technological (A.8). Map checklist findings to the Annex A controls below. Use category-level references; do not fabricate control numbers beyond this list.

## Access control

- **A.5.15 – Access control.** Rules for logical and physical access. → access control.
- **A.5.16 – Identity management.** → access control.
- **A.5.17 – Authentication information.** Manage secrets/credentials. → access control, encryption (secrets).
- **A.5.18 – Access rights.** Provisioning/review/removal, least privilege. → access control.
- **A.8.2 – Privileged access rights.** → access control.
- **A.8.5 – Secure authentication.** MFA/SSO. → access control.

## Change management

- **A.8.32 – Change management.** Changes to information systems are controlled. → change management.
- **A.8.25 – Secure development lifecycle.** → change management.
- **A.8.28 – Secure coding.** → change management, standards.
- **A.8.31 – Separation of dev/test/prod environments.** → change management.

## Logging & monitoring

- **A.8.15 – Logging.** Produce, store, and protect logs of activities. → logging & monitoring.
- **A.8.16 – Monitoring activities.** Detect anomalous behavior. → logging & monitoring.

## Encryption

- **A.8.24 – Use of cryptography.** Policy for crypto and key management. → encryption.
- **A.5.34 – Privacy and protection of PII.** → data handling, encryption.

## Incident response

- **A.5.24 – Incident management planning and preparation.** → incident response.
- **A.5.25 – Assessment and decision on events.** → incident response.
- **A.5.26 – Response to incidents.** → incident response.
- **A.8.13 – Information backup.** → incident response (backup/restore).
- **A.5.30 – ICT readiness for business continuity.** → incident response.

## Usage notes

- Cite the Annex A control at identifier level (e.g. "A.8.24 – use of cryptography").
- ISO 27001 certification also requires the clause 4–10 ISMS management system; this checklist covers Annex A technical/operational controls only, not the full ISMS. Note that boundary when reporting audit readiness.
